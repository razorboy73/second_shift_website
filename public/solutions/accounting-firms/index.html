Busy season shouldn’t mean inbox season.

  • Referrals are your best source of new clients, but nobody has time to ask for them.
  • New inquiries sit in the inbox for days while everyone is heads-down on returns.
  • You send the same “we still need your 1099s” email forty times every March.
  • Receipts and uncategorized transactions pile up until month-end.
  • Staff spend hours each month assembling the same reports for every client.
  • “Where’s my return?” calls interrupt focused work all day.

Where AI helps, and where you stay in charge.

Each workflow runs on the tools you already use. Start with one, and add more once it’s paying off.

Win more clients

  • Inquiry response & qualification

    New inquiries get a reply in minutes. AI asks the right questions (entity type, services, timing) and books qualified prospects straight into your calendar.

    Human check: Anything unclear is routed to a partner.

  • Proposals & engagement letters

    AI drafts the proposal from your discovery-call notes. When the client signs, their folder, portal invite and onboarding checklist are created automatically.

    Human check: You set the scope and the fee.

  • Referral & review requests

    After a return is filed, the client gets a personal thank-you and a request for a referral or review.

    Human check: You approve the wording once.

Run a tighter operation

  • Document collection that reads

    AI recognizes uploaded W-2s, 1099s and K-1s, files them, updates the checklist and keeps reminding until the file is complete.

    Human check: Unreadable or unexpected documents go to a person.

  • Bookkeeping prep

    Receipts and transactions categorized using each client’s history. Anything that doesn’t fit the pattern is flagged.

    Human check: A person decides every flagged item.

  • Monthly reporting packs

    Figures pulled from QuickBooks or Xero and a first draft of the commentary written, so reviewing each report takes minutes.

    Human check: Nothing reaches a client before you sign off.

Common questions.

Is it safe to put client financial data through AI?

Yes, with the right setup. We use business-grade AI services configured so your data isn’t used to train models, limit access to what each workflow needs, and keep a person reviewing anything that reaches a client.

What if the AI gets a number wrong?

That’s why AI drafts and a person approves. We design each workflow so mistakes get caught before they matter, flag low-confidence results for review, and track accuracy over time.

Can we get something in place before tax season?

Yes, if we start early and start focused. Document collection and inquiry response are usually the best first steps. We plan backwards from your deadlines.
