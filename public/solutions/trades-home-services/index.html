  • Calls come in while you’re under a sink or on a mower, and the customer books whoever picks up.

  • Invoices go out days after the job, and chasing payment is awkward.

Where AI helps, and where you stay in charge.

Each workflow runs on the tools you already use. Start with one, and add more once it’s paying off.

Win more clients

  • Missed calls answered

    Missed calls get an instant text. AI answers questions, collects the details and books the job into your schedule.

    Human check: Emergencies go straight to whoever’s on call.

  • Estimates from voice notes

    Talk through the job on site; AI turns it into a clean estimate, then follows up on day 2 and day 7 until there’s an answer.

    Human check: You set every price.

  • Reviews & repeat business

    Review requests after every paid job, plus seasonal reminders (spring aeration, furnace tune-ups) to past customers.

    Human check: Only sent after a job is paid and closed.

Run a tighter operation

  • Invoice on completion

    Closing a job in your field app sends the invoice with a payment link, followed by polite reminders if it goes unpaid.

    Human check: Disputed invoices are flagged to you.

  • Scheduling & weather delays

    Recurring routes and maintenance plans scheduled automatically. Rain day? Everyone affected is notified and rebooked in one step.

    Human check: You confirm the reschedule.

  • Contractor coordination

    AI turns daily notes and photos into client progress updates, and pushes schedule changes to subs and suppliers.

    Human check: You approve updates and change orders.

Common questions.

Will customers know they’re texting with AI?

We recommend saying so in the first message. Most customers just want a fast answer and a booked time, and anything the assistant can’t handle is passed to you.

My crew isn’t into technology. Do they need to learn something new?

Very little. Most of it runs in the background. Anything your crew touches works by text message or the field app they already use.

Do you work with general contractors as well as service trades?

Yes. For contractors we focus on client progress updates, sub and supplier coordination, change orders and estimating.
