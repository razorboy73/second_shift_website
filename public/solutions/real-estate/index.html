The deals are in the follow-up. So is the busywork.

  • Leads from portals, your website and open houses land in different places, and some never get a reply.
  • Prospecting gets squeezed out by the transactions you already have.
  • Past clients hear from you once a year, so their referrals go to the agent who stayed in touch.
  • Every listing means writing the same description, posts and emails from scratch.
  • Every transaction means tracking a dozen dates across inspection, appraisal, financing and closing.
  • Showing feedback and seller updates eat an evening every week.

Where AI helps, and where you stay in charge.

Each workflow runs on the tools you already use. Start with one, and add more once it’s paying off.

Win more clients

  • Speed-to-lead response

    Every new lead gets an immediate, personal reply. AI answers questions about the property, qualifies on timing and financing, and books the showing.

    Human check: You take over the moment a lead is ready to talk.

  • Prospecting research & outreach

    Expired listings, FSBOs and your farm area researched for you, with personal letters or emails drafted for each owner.

    Human check: You approve outreach. Opt-outs and your brokerage’s rules are respected.

  • Past-client & sphere nurture

    Personal market updates about each client’s own home and neighborhood, sent on a steady rhythm so you stay the agent they recommend.

    Human check: Written in your voice from templates you approve.

Run a tighter operation

  • Listing content in minutes

    From your notes and photos, AI drafts the listing description, social posts, email announcement and property page.

    Human check: You edit and approve before anything is published.

  • Transaction timelines

    AI reads the purchase agreement, pulls out the key dates and builds a timeline with reminders to you, your clients, the lender and title.

    Human check: You or your TC confirm every date.

  • Showing feedback & seller updates

    Feedback requested after every showing, then summarized into a weekly update your sellers will actually read.

    Human check: Sent after a quick read-through.

Common questions.

Will leads know they’re talking to AI?

We recommend being upfront that an assistant handles first replies, and many buyers appreciate the instant answer. Either way, messages are written in your voice and hand the conversation to you as soon as a lead is ready.

Is AI-written listing copy compliant?

AI produces the first draft; you stay responsible for the final version. We set it up with your brokerage’s guidelines and fair housing language rules, and nothing publishes without your approval.

Does this work for a solo agent?

Often best of all. A solo agent has nobody to catch the lead that came in during a showing. Lead response and past-client nurture usually pay off first.
