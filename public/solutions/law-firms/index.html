Your time is billable. The admin around it isn’t.

  • Prospective clients wait days for a callback while intake forms sit in a shared inbox.
  • Consultations that don’t retain you never get a follow-up.
  • Conflict checks and new-matter setup take staff time on every inquiry, including the ones you decline.
  • Attorneys rebuild the same letters and agreements from old files.
  • Time gets reconstructed at the end of the week, and some never gets billed.
  • Clients call for status updates that a short email could have answered.

Where AI helps, and where you stay in charge.

Each workflow runs on the tools you already use. Start with one, and add more once it’s paying off.

Win more clients

  • Intake & qualification

    AI reads each inquiry, sorts it by practice area and urgency, gathers the facts you need and books a consultation with the right attorney.

    Human check: No legal advice is given. An attorney reviews every new matter.

  • Consultation follow-up

    Prospects who haven’t retained you yet get timely, courteous follow-up instead of silence.

    Human check: Templates approved by an attorney.

  • Referral partner updates

    AI drafts short updates on relevant legal changes for the accountants, advisors and past clients who send you work.

    Human check: An attorney reviews content before it goes out.

Run a tighter operation

  • Conflict check preparation

    Names and parties from each inquiry checked against existing matters, with likely matches surfaced and explained.

    Human check: An attorney clears every conflict.

  • First drafts from your templates

    Engagement letters, routine correspondence and standard agreements drafted from matter data and your own precedents.

    Human check: Attorney review before anything is sent or filed.

  • Time capture

    AI suggests time entries from calendars, emails and documents worked on, so the week doesn’t need reconstructing.

    Human check: Attorneys confirm entries before billing.

Common questions.

How do you handle confidentiality with AI?

We design around your professional obligations: business-grade AI services configured so client data isn’t used for training, access limited to what each workflow needs, and an attorney reviewing every client-facing draft. Legal judgment stays with your attorneys.

Will AI give legal advice to prospects?

No. Intake workflows gather facts, answer logistical questions and book consultations. Anything that looks like a request for advice is routed to an attorney.

Where should a small firm start?

Intake. It affects revenue directly, and it frees up the staff time that every other improvement depends on. A readiness review will confirm whether that’s right for you.
