Every empty chair is lost revenue. Every message is a pause.

  • Instagram and website messages asking about prices and availability go unanswered for hours.
  • No-shows and late cancellations leave expensive gaps in the book.
  • Regulars drift away because nobody reminded them it’s been eight weeks.
  • Happy clients walk out without being asked for a review.
  • Retail products run out before anyone notices.
  • Commissions and tips are tallied in a spreadsheet every pay period.

Where AI helps, and where you stay in charge.

Each workflow runs on the tools you already use. Start with one, and add more once it’s paying off.

Win more clients

  • AI booking assistant

    Answers price, service and availability questions on Instagram, Facebook and your website, and books the appointment.

    Human check: Tricky requests go to the front desk.

  • Rebooking & win-back

    Clients are nudged to rebook based on their service, and lapsed regulars get a personal note that sounds like you.

    Human check: You approve offers and wording.

  • Reviews & referrals

    A thank-you and review request after each visit, with a referral offer for your most loyal clients.

    Human check: Unhappy feedback comes to you first.

Run a tighter operation

  • Confirmations & waitlist fill

    Confirmations and reminders with your deposit policy, and open slots offered to matching waitlist clients automatically.

    Human check: Deposits and policies set by you.

  • Retail stock alerts

    Low-stock alerts and draft reorders when products drop below the levels you set.

    Human check: You place the order.

  • Payroll prep

    Commission and tip summaries compiled per stylist each pay period.

    Human check: Checked by you before payroll.

Common questions.

Will clients find AI replies impersonal?

Not when they sound like you. We train the assistant on your services, prices and tone, keep replies short, and hand anything personal or tricky to your team.

We already use booking software with reminders. What else is there?

Booking tools handle the basics. We add what they leave out: answering messages on social, win-back campaigns, review follow-up, stock alerts and payroll prep.

Does this work for a single-chair studio?

Yes. Solo stylists often benefit most, because there’s no front desk to fall back on.
