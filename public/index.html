Two places AI pays off fastest.

Win more clients

Most small businesses don’t lose work to competitors. They lose it to slow replies and follow-ups nobody had time to send.

  • Find prospects worth contactingAI researches lists like expired listings, new businesses in your area or old inquiries, and surfaces the ones that fit.
  • Personal outreach without the hoursA first draft written for each prospect, approved by you before anything is sent.
  • Every inquiry answered in minutesAI replies, asks the qualifying questions and books the call, including evenings and weekends.
  • Follow-up until there’s an answerQuotes and proposals chased politely and on schedule, so deals don’t go quiet.
  • Referrals and reviews, on cueHappy clients asked at the right moment, every time.

Run a tighter operation

The other half of the week goes to work that keeps the lights on: reading, typing, sorting, chasing and reporting.

  • Documents read for youInvoices, receipts, contracts and forms read, sorted and entered into the right system.

  • Systems that stay in syncDetails entered once and updated everywhere: CRM, accounting, scheduling.
  • Scheduling, reminders and invoicingThe routine admin that runs on its own once it’s set up properly.

Questions we hear a lot.

What does using AI “properly” actually mean?

Starting with a real business problem, choosing the right tool for it, protecting your data, putting a person’s review wherever a mistake would be costly, and measuring whether it actually saves time or wins business. If it doesn’t, we change it or switch it off.

Is our client data safe with AI?

We use business-grade AI services configured so your data isn’t used to train models, limit each workflow’s access to what it needs, and document where data goes. For regulated work like legal and accounting, we design around your professional obligations.

Will AI replace my staff?

That isn’t the goal. The aim is to take repetitive work off your team so the people you have can spend more time with clients and handle more work.

Which AI tools do you use?

Whatever fits the job: models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft, the AI features already built into your software, and automation platforms such as Zapier, Make or n8n to connect them. We’re not tied to any vendor.

Do we need to be technical?

No. We handle the technical side and train your team on the parts they’ll use. Most workflows run in the background and show up in the email, text or software you already use.

Where should we start?

Usually with an AI readiness review. It shows where AI will help most in your business, what to do first and what it will cost, before you commit to building anything.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope. After our first conversation you’ll get a clear quote, so you can weigh it against the hours and business you’ll get back.
